A full room atmosphere overhaul mod for **!Ω Factorial Omega: My Dystopian Robot Girlfriend (MDRG)** bringing a sleek gaming room aesthetic to your apartment.

---

## 🎮 Features

- **Full Suite of Furniture & Decor:**
  - Custom Wall/Room Background (`bg.png`)
  - Gaming Desk & Chair (`desk.png`, `couch.png`)
  - Gaming PC Setup with RGB lighting (`PC.png`, `PCrgb.png`)
  - Flatscreen TV & Cupboard (`TV.png`, `cupboard.png`)
  - Modern Bed, Plushy & Rug (`bed.png`, `plushy.png`, `rug.png`)
  - Custom Door & Window frames
- **Modular Scripts:** Separate Lua configuration scripts for each piece of furniture.
- Verified 100% working on MDRG version **v0.97.12** (Target: `0.97.11`).

---

## 📥 Installation

1. Go to the [**Releases**](https://github.com/MDRG-MODS/gaming-room/releases) page and download `Gaming Room.zip`.
2. Navigate to your MDRG installation directory:
   ```text
   .../My Dystopian Robot Girlfriend/FactorialOmega_Data/StreamingAssets/Mods/
   ```
3. Extract `Gaming Room.zip` into your `Mods/` directory so that `mod.json` is located at:
   ```text
   .../StreamingAssets/Mods/Gaming Room/mod.json
   ```
4. Launch the game and decorate your room via the in-game room customization menu.
