A full room atmosphere overhaul mod for **!Ω Factorial Omega: My Dystopian Robot Girlfriend (MDRG)** bringing a sleek gaming room aesthetic to your apartment.

---

## 🎮 Features

- **Full Suite of Furniture & Decor:** Custom background (`bg.png`), Gaming Desk & Couch (`desk.png`, `couch.png`), Gaming PC with RGB (`PC.png`, `PCrgb.png`), TV, Bed, Plushy, Rug, and decor.
- **Modular Scripts:** Separate Lua configuration scripts for each piece of furniture.
- Compatible with MDRG version **v0.97.12** (Target: `0.97.11`).

---

## ⚙️ Mod Type

This is an **Internal Mod**, designed to be imported, enabled, and managed directly using MDRG's built-in in-game mod manager (available on PC, Mac, and Android). It does not require external modding tools or loaders such as LemonLoader.

Ready-to-use mod packages are available under the [**Releases**](https://github.com/MDRG-MODS/gaming-room/releases) tab.
